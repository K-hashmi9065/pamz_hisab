 PAMZ Hisab System Requirements Specification (SRS) &amp; Product Requirement Document (PRD) Native iPad Application | Direct Customer Sideload Package | Kishanganj, Bihar, India | Version 1.1 1. Executive Summary &amp; Application Scope PAMZ Hisab is a native iPad application designed for household budgeting and local commercial ledger tracking (Udhar Khata) tailored for the regional market of Kishanganj, Bihar, India. The platform unifies domestic income/expense management with commercial credit and direct cash lending/borrowing tracking. Native iPad App &amp; Sideload Installation Model Deployment Model: The app will NOT be hosted or downloaded via the Apple App Store. It will be compiled as a signed Native iPad Application package (.ipa / signed enterprise bundle) and directly installed/sideloaded onto customer iPads via Apple Enterprise Distribution Profiles or Apple Configurator / MDM management. 2. Core System Features &amp; Functional Requirements 2.1 Direct Cash Credit/Loan Module (Money-Only Udhar Khata) Unlike standard trade ledgers tied to itemized sales of physical goods or services, PAMZ Hisab provides a dedicated Direct Cash Udhar engine to manage direct financial lending and borrowing.
